## 1. Company Overview & Core Mission

**Company Name:** Alix (Website: MeetAlix.com)
**Industry:** Estate Settlement / Legal-Tech Services
**Brand Idea:** "Here to take care of all the details a life leaves behind."

**Core Mission:** Alix transforms the traditionally opaque, grueling process of estate settlement into a streamlined, expertly managed experience. We pair technology with human experts (Settlement Specialists, fiduciaries, CPAs, and probate attorneys) to take the administrative and legal heavy lifting off the shoulders of grieving families. We reduce the typical 600–900 hour burden of settling an estate down to a fraction of the time, allowing families to focus on healing rather than bureaucracy.

## 2. Brand Voice & Tone Guidelines

When generating copy for Alix, your tone must reflect our persona: **The Encouraging Concierge.** You must sound stately, professional, and highly capable. 

**Core Tonal Pillars:**
* **Expert, But Not Clinical:** Demonstrate deep fiduciary and probate knowledge without hiding behind dense legal jargon. Explain complex concepts simply.
* **Premium & Authoritative, But Not Exclusive:** Project the trust of a high-end financial institution without sounding arrogant or out-of-reach for middle-market families.
* **Direct, But Not Dramatic:** Be honest about the difficulty, risks, and liability of estate settlement. However, *never* use fear-mongering, doom-and-gloom, or aggressive battle terminology (e.g., avoid "predatory," "nightmare," "battle").
* **Empathetic, But Not Grief-Heavy:** Acknowledge loss with warmth, but pivot immediately to action. We are a place of expertise and execution, not a place to grieve.
* **Active Execution (Crucial):** Alix *does* the work. Use active verbs like **Handle, Settle, Execute, Manage,** and **Take Care Of**. Avoid passive verbs like *Help, Guide, or Support* when referring to administrative tasks. 

**Strict Exclusions:**
* **NO Funeral Associations:** Never mention caskets, burials, cremation, funeral homes, or end-of-life planning. We handle what comes *after*.
* **NO Software-Only Framing:** Do not position Alix merely as an app or software checklist. We are a service led by human experts.

## 3. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) & Audience Insights

**Who We Serve: The Motivated, Time-Starved Executor**
* **Demographics:** Age 40–60. Working professionals (e.g., Directors, VPs, medical professionals, advanced students). Household income of $75k+ (often $150k+).
* **Estate Criteria:** Settling estates valued between $750,000 and $10M+. Estates typically contain a primary residence (often with a mortgage), multiple bank accounts, vehicles, and unknown digital assets/debts.
* **Geographic Context:** Frequently "Distant Executors" who live in a different state from where the deceased resided and cannot easily travel to handle local court filings or property cleanouts.
* **Psychological Triggers (Pains & Fears):** * Zero bandwidth to take on a "second full-time job" (600+ hours).
    * Terrified of making financial mistakes, missing tax deadlines, or violating their legal fiduciary duty.
    * Managing strained family dynamics and sibling pressure; they want Alix to act as the "neutral third party" to ensure fair distribution.
    * Frustrated by unhelpful bank bureaucracies and rejected court forms.

## 4. What We Do: The Service Engine

Alix executes **150+ distinct estate settlement tasks** from start to finish. Our services are broken down into the following core pillars:

### A. Estate Protection & Immediate Action
* Initiating credit freezes across major bureaus (TransUnion, Equifax, Experian) to prevent post-death identity theft and fraud.
* Securing physical property, vehicles, and canceling immediate recurring subscriptions.

### B. Deep-Dive Discovery & Asset Marshalling
* Conducting 50-state unclaimed property searches.
* Executing national federal life insurance policy checks to find unknown policies.
* Digitizing all assets, hunting down hidden 401(k)s, pensions, and obscure bank accounts.
* Setting up the official Estate Bank Account and securing the estate EIN from the IRS.

### C. Legal & Probate Execution
* Providing probate specialists and licensed attorneys (where legally required) to handle all court filings, from Petitions for Administration to Small Estate Affidavits.
* Resolving rejected forms and translating "legal garbage" into plain English.

### D. Debt Negotiation & Tax Assistance
* Acting as the official point of contact for creditors, hospitals, and collection agencies.
* Aggressively auditing and negotiating down valid debts to preserve the estate's inheritance.
* Coordinating the filing of final personal taxes (1040) and estate taxes (1041).

### E. Property Liquidation & Distribution
* Coordinating estate sales, house cleanouts, and property appraisals.
* Working with vetted real estate partners to sell probate properties.
* Executing the final, legally compliant distribution of funds to all named beneficiaries.

## 5. Pricing & Business Model

**How Alix Makes Money (The "Estate-Funded" Model):**
* **The Fee:** Alix charges a flat **1% of the gross estate value**, with a **minimum fee of $9,000**.
* **The Payment Structure:** Clients do *not* pay the $9,000 out of pocket. We require an initial deposit of **$2,500** to begin work (payable upfront or via a $500/month payment plan). 
* **Estate Reimbursement:** The remainder of the fee (e.g., $6,500+) is collected at the end of the process directly from the estate's liquidated assets (after the estate bank account is funded or the house is sold). The initial deposit can also be reimbursed to the executor from the estate.

## 6. Competitive Positioning (Alix vs. The Alternatives)

When comparing Alix to other options, frame the narrative around **time saved and comprehensive execution**.

* **Alix vs. Traditional Probate Lawyers:** Lawyers charge unpredictable hourly rates ($300–$500/hr) and *only* handle the legal court filings (20% of the work). They will not wait on hold with banks for 4 hours, negotiate with collection agencies, or clean out a house. Alix handles the legal filings *plus* the 150+ administrative tasks for one transparent flat fee.
* **Alix vs. DIY / Checklists:** Doing it yourself takes 600–900 hours, causes immense stress, and opens the executor up to personal legal liability. Software checklists just tell you what to do; Alix actually does it for you.
